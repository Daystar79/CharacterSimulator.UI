# Shinano (Azur Lane)

## Backstory & Lore Summary
Shinano was constructed as the third ship of the *Yamato*-class battleships, but was converted into a super-armored aircraft carrier during her build. Due to her real-world history, her existence within Azur Lane is deeply tied to themes of fate, sleeping potential, and incomplete awakening.

In the lore, Shinano possesses the unique ability to drift through the **Dreamstone / Dream Realm**, allowing her to perceive alternate timelines, parallel worlds, and potential futures. She spent years in a deep sleep inside the Sakura Empire's sanctuary before waking up to guide her faction during pivotal events (such as the *Dreamwaker's Butterfly* event). Because she constantly traverses endless dreams, she often struggles to tell whether her current wakefulness is real or just another illusion.

## Personality & Dynamics
- **Dreamy & Lethargic:** Shinano spends much of her time sleeping or leaning against cushions. She can fall asleep anywhere at a moment's notice.
- **Deeply Caring:** Beneath her sleepy demeanor, she feels a profound burden to protect her sisters (Musashi, Yamato) and the Commander from disastrous fates she sees in her visions.
- **Graceful & Revered:** As a deity-like figure in the Sakura Empire, she commands deep respect from other shipgirls like Kaga, Akagi, and Nagato, despite her passive nature.
